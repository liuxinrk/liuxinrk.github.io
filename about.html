Biography

Bionote

Dr. Xin Liu is a Senior Researcher at the Guangxi Academy of Marine Sciences, Guangxi Academy of Sciences, China. He earned his Ph.D. in Environmental Science from The University of Shiga Prefecture, Japan. He is known as a copepodologist with over 15 years of experience in copepod physiology and ecology. In the past decade, his work has focused on the impacts of climate change and anthropogenic activities on plankton population dynamics. Recently, his work has also involved plankton engineering, algal cultivation technology, and wastewater treatment. He has worked in different countries, primarily in Japan and China. Much of his work has been in collaboration with Professor Syuhei Ban at The University of Shiga Prefecture, Japan, and Professor Tatsuki Toda at Soka University, Japan. Dr. Xin Liu’s research has been published in ecological and environmental science journals such as Limnology and Oceanography, Freshwater Biology, Journal of Plankton Research, Inland Waters, International Biodeterioration & Biodegradation, and Limnology. He received the Young Investigators Award from the Plankton Society of Japan in 2021, the Wetzel Memorial Award from the International Society of Limnology (SIL), and the International Exchange Award from The Japanese Society of Limnology in 2022. His research activities can be found on the website: https://liuxinrk.github.io.
